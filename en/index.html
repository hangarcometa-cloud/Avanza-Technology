With Sofia

07 / FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to change my number?

No. You can forward only the calls you miss, or only after-hours calls. If you want a new number for ads, we can set that up too.

Will my customers know it’s AI?

Sofia never pretends to be a person. If someone asks, she says she’s your company’s virtual assistant and that a team member will follow up.

Does she really handle both languages?

She switches the moment the caller does, even mid-call. The best way to judge is to request the demo and try both.

What happens in an emergency?

She gives safety instructions first, like leaving the home and calling 911 if there’s a gas smell. Then she takes the details and sends you the call flagged as urgent.

What do I need to get started?

A 30-minute video call so we learn your services, service area, hours and how you schedule. We handle the rest.

Where are you based?

We’re a remote team working with businesses in Texas. Everything happens by phone, WhatsApp and video call in Spanish, and by WhatsApp or email in English.
